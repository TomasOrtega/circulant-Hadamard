# Proof exposition

Shared vocabulary for navigating and explaining the proof.

## Language

**Node**:

A unit of the proof exposition, with an overview video, a written explanation,
and optional child nodes. This is the standard term at every depth.
_Avoid_: Lesson, chapter, subchapter.

**Parent node**:

A higher-level explanation that introduces a node.

**Child node**:

A more detailed explanation of one part of its parent node.

**Dependency**:

A relationship in which one node uses material established by another. An arrow
points from the prerequisite to the node that uses it.

**Overview video**:

The full video for a node, introducing and explaining its child nodes in order.

**Cut**:

A bounded excerpt from an overview video, used to preview a child node.

**Script**:

The narration that guides a video's written explanation. A cut's explanation
follows the corresponding part of its parent's script.

**Foundation node**:

A node at the level of standard undergraduate prerequisites, with no further
child explanations.
