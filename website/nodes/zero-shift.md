Every squared entry equals 1, so this sum has n terms equal to 1. This accounts for the diagonal of $HH^{\mathsf{T}}$.

The diagonal is fixed automatically. The nonzero shifts carry the remaining restrictions.

$$
C(0) = \sum_{j=0}^{n-1} a_j^2 = n.
$$
